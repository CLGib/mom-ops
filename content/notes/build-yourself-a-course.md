Last year, my daughter started kindergarten.

Such an exciting time. My baby, heading off into the world of big kids.

To me, kindergarten is the year you set the precedents. This is what our family is about. This is what we expect of ourselves.

And here's one of ours: we don't sit around waiting for someday. We set goals and we go make them happen. Someday isn't a day. TODAY is. So what's the baby step we can take right now to get one step closer?

So the week before school, we had Vision Board Night.

I gave her categories. Health. Life skills. Community. For each one, she had to set a goal and come up with a plan to actually reach it.

It's really an exercise in owning your own story. At five years old.

Now, I've always been a "goals" person, and honestly, I usually reach them.

But this year something wild happened.

Because I saw my board every single day, I hit most of my goals in under three months. All of them within the year. So I started adjusting it every three months or so. New goals, look at it daily, and boom. Hit them again.

And every time, my confidence skyrocketed. Because if I could do that, what other limiting belief was I just believing for no reason? Ya know?

So this year, first grade starts, Vision Board Night rolls around again, and I'd just finished my honest postmortem on Mom Ops ([that was last week, catch up here](/notes/the-pivot)).

I knew what my next mountain was. Content strategy. I'm bad at it, and it's the thing standing in my way.

But here's the trap. We live in a world where every piece of information you could ever want is right at your fingertips. And somehow that makes it HARDER. You get so overwhelmed by the pile that you do nothing at all.

I almost signed up for some guided class. Not because I needed it. Just to make the decision fatigue go away.

And then it hit me.

What if I build my own guided class? A real one. An actual syllabus, with actual deliverables. (Ahem. Kind of like the thing you're reading right now. Hehe.)

THIS. This is what AI is actually for. Not doing the work for you. Helping you build the thing that makes YOU do the work.

So yeah. I'm now enrolled in my own Gibson Growth Academy.

Here's how to enroll yourself.

## How to build your own academy

**1. Name your mountain.** The one thing you're bad at that's actually in your way. Mine is content. Say it out loud. It stings less than you think.

**2. Turn it into a syllabus, not a vibe.** This is the AI part. Ask it to act like a curriculum designer and build you a real course, from where you are now to genuinely good. Modules, in order, one thing to practice each week. Then push back until it fits YOU and not some imaginary student.

**3. Make the homework something you ship.** Not notes. Not "learn about content." Actual delivered things. My final project is this newsletter, published every week, good or not.

**4. Put it where you'll see it every day.** This is the whole vision board secret. The goals I looked at daily are the ones I hit. A course sitting in a folder you never open does nothing.

**5. Adjust every few months.** Hit the goals, move the board, pick the next mountain. That's the whole loop.

That's it. That's the Academy.
